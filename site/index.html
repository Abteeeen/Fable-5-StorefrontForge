How it works

Three steps. You only do the first one.

The whole process runs on our production pipeline — you never touch a website builder.

1

You submit

Product photos (or a 10-second video of each product slowly rotating on a table), one sentence about your business, and your WhatsApp number. That's the entire brief.

2

We forge

Our pipeline designs your brand, writes your copy, builds interactive 360° viewers from your photos, and assembles the store. You approve a preview link before anything goes public.

3

You launch

Your store goes live at its own URL with order buttons wired to your WhatsApp — plus a launch ad kit: scored, ready-to-post creatives for Instagram and stories.

Get your store

Submit your request

Have these three things ready — the form takes about five minutes:

  1. One sentence about your business — what you sell, the vibe, how you take orders.
  2. Product photos — clear shots on a plain background. For the 360° effect, add a short video of each product slowly rotating on a table (a lazy susan or even a slow hand-turn works).
  3. Your WhatsApp number — where order buttons should point, and where we send your preview link.

We reply with a preview link. Nothing goes live until you approve it.
